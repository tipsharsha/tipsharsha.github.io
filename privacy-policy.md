# Crownfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Crownfall is a logic puzzle game for Android published by HarshStudios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

## The short version

- Crownfall has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no servers.
- Your progress, settings, streaks and statistics stay on your device.
- We do not use any analytics or tracking tools of our own.
- The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.
- A one-time purchase removes all ads.

## Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any personal information. Crownfall works fully offline. Puzzles are generated on your device, including the daily puzzle, which is worked out from the calendar date.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device: your settings, which puzzles you have solved and your times, your daily streak, unfinished games, your hint balance, and whether you have bought Remove Ads. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include app data in your own device backup.

## Advertising

The free version of Crownfall shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. There are two kinds: full-screen ads that can appear between level-pack puzzles, and optional video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for hints or to open a pack early. No ad is shown while you are solving a puzzle, and none on the daily puzzle.

To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect and send to Google:

- the advertising ID of your device, where available;
- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
- device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
- information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the SDK.

Google uses this to serve ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud, and, where you have agreed, to personalise ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Your consent and choices

- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can refuse. You can change your decision at any time under Settings, Privacy choices.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings under Google, Ads.
- You can remove ads completely with the one-time Remove Ads purchase. After that purchase the app no longer requests ads.

## Purchases

Remove Ads is sold through Google Play Billing. The payment is handled entirely by Google. We never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase belongs to your account.

## Sharing results

When you choose Share, the app creates a short text and an image of your result and passes them to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you start it.

## Permissions

Crownfall requests internet access and network state, the advertising ID and Google's ad-services permissions, and Google Play Billing. These are used for ads and the Remove Ads purchase only. The ads and audio components also declare permissions to keep the device awake while an ad or sound plays. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Children

Crownfall is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To erase the data on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
